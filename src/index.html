    PROCUREMENT EDITION 3.1

    Story & procurement rules

    Manage procurement exception alerts through planned supplier corrective action, supplier risk review, or a quality hold with immediate corrective action. The manufacturing guilds remain the supply network you coordinate.

    Ironhold's six guilds build a force of Titan’s Bastion war golems. The Emberborn forge frames; the Gearwrights build engines. Hammer and Anvil combines these into subassemblies while the Arcane Weavers enchant armor. The Titanwrights integrate the parts, and the Order of the Unerring Gaze approves each finished Titan.

    Early Dawn: rounds 1–5. Fires of Noon: 6–10. Twilight of Battle: 11–15. All teams operate every round. Cycle boundaries do not reset cash, stock or repairs.

    1. Objective and setup

    Each team starts with 10 gold by default; the Game Master can choose 1–1000 gold per guild before starting. A round is an 8-hour day. Full capacity is two whole units. All five buffers begin at zero; raw materials are unlimited apart from vendor incidents. Select practice mode or announce a whole-game target of 1–30 approved Titans before starting.

    A mission succeeds only if all six teams remain above zero gold and meet the target by round 15, including final settlement. Only then do the richest guilds share the Master Guild title. Zero or negative gold ends play. Practice mode reports output and survival without a target victory. Thirty shipments is the physical upper bound, not a tested target.

    2. Stages, capacity and inventory

    Stage 1: Shops 1 and 2. Stage 2: Shop 3 and Subassembly. Stage 3: Final Assembly. Stage 4: Quality. Paired teams release output together, after both decisions are complete. Resolve their events in Shop 1 then Shop 2 order, and Shop 3 then Subassembly order.

    At round start, resolve due analysis tasks, then serve up to 8 queued repair hours per team. A team serving repairs makes no production or event roll; its base capacity is 2. Other teams with a complete input set roll d6: 1–3 gives base capacity 1; 4–6 gives 2. No input set means no roll and no fine.

    Usable capacity = max(0, base capacity − repair hours served ÷ 4). Actual output is the minimum of usable capacity and each required input buffer. Consume one of each required input per good output unit. Carry unused tokens forward. Shops have no input constraint. Only quality-approved units count as shipments. A zero-hour fix does not restore the unit lost on a partial production roll.

    Immediate repair hours are served before that team's output is released. Future tickets wait until their due round. Hours are served sequentially, never more than 8 per team per round. The app pauses before each stage's output release so the class can predict the result.

    3. Procurement exceptions and responses
    d10CauseApplies to
    OddUnknownReroll until an applicable even result
    2Machine failureAll teams
    4Defective partSubassembly, final assembly, quality
    6MisalignmentAll teams
    8Defective raw materialShops 1–3 outside vendor protection
    10BreakdownAll teams

    Roll an event only after partial production. Reroll inapplicable events without a charge. A physical d10 marked 0 means 10; enter 10 in the app.

    Supply clear: healthy local process. Planned supplier corrective action: choose a solution now, pay now, repair next round. Supplier risk review: resolve a d6 analysis next round. Quality hold: immediate corrective action. Only failure and misalignment allow planned corrective action or risk review. Defective parts and breakdowns require a quality hold. Vendor incidents use the vendor rule. Input starvation is Idle. Procurement exception alerts carry no extra fee.

    Analysis d6: 1–2 = 0 hours / 0 gold, 3–4 = 4 hours / 1 gold, 5–6 = 8 hours / 2 gold. The incident then closes. Round-15 analysis is settled after production ends; it cannot evade its charge.

    4. Repairs, rework and costs
    d4SolutionApplicable causeTicket
    1Stop and investigateFailure or misalignment4h / 1 gold
    2Root causeAny non-vendor incident8h / 2 gold
    3Software / parts fixFailure or misalignment0h / 0 gold
    4ReworkDefective part8h / 2 gold to origin

    Reroll inapplicable solutions. Solutions 1–3 belong to the detecting team; quality hold is immediate; planned corrective action starts next round. A free fix closes immediately.

    Rework holds the detecting team's output at zero and retains its inputs. That team pays no extra repair fee. For a defect at Subassembly, origin d6 1–3 assigns Shop 1 and 4–6 Shop 2. At Final Assembly, 1–3 assigns Subassembly and 4–6 Shop 3. At Quality, the origin is Final Assembly without a die. The origin pays now and serves 8 hours from next round.

    Each ticket is charged once when issued: 4h = 1 gold; 8h = 2; forced-vendor 32h = 7. Four separate 8h tickets cost 8, not 7. Serving already-paid repair hours does not charge again. Partial capacity, starvation and an exception alert do not create separate monetary fines.

    The app checks bankruptcy after each resolved charge or atomic batch of charges. All three charges of one vendor change are posted together. Due analyses are resolved as one round-opening batch. If play ends before a stage release, no staged output is transferred.

    5. Vendor vote and protection

    Only a valid event 8 triggers this rule. Count occurrences across all three shops for the current vendor. At occurrence 1 or 2, all three shops vote; two votes to change trigger an 8h / 2-gold ticket for each shop. Otherwise keep the vendor and the count. The affected shop retains partial base capacity 1 this round; there is no local solution roll.

    Occurrence 3 forces a change: 32h / 7 gold per shop, without an additional voluntary charge. Vendor repairs begin next round. Reset the count on any change. Protection begins immediately and covers the next five numbered rounds: change in round 4 protects through round 9; event 8 becomes eligible in round 10. Reroll raw-material events while protected. Current-round production is not reversed.

    6. Gold transfers and Game Master exceptions

    While a game is active, guilds can transfer whole gold amounts with a recorded agreement. The sending guild must retain at least 1 gold. Transfers conserve total gold and affect the richest-guild ranking. Coordinate before a charge: transfers cannot revive a game already ended by bankruptcy.

    The Game Master can grant or deduct gold immediately, add free downtime for the next round (rounds 1–14 only), or record a custom instruction. Notes do not change game calculations. Every exception requires an instructor name and explanation. A penalty reaching zero gold ends play. These shared-screen controls are not authenticated accounts.

    7. Ending, saving and teaching

    After round 15, resolve all deferred analysis before judging the outcome. Already-paid repair hours remaining beyond the game are disclosed, not charged again. No extra shipments are permitted. At cycle breaks, discuss the bottleneck, the value of a warning, and local gold versus factory output.

    Save game downloads a JSON history. Load replays and validates it; it does not trust editable balances. Browser autosave is a convenience, not a backup. Use Undo only to correct an input error, not to reroll an unfavorable result. CSV contains team-round accounting. Unreleased rows are explicitly marked. The history retains every die, reroll and vote.

    This is a simplified teaching model: defects reduce or hold output; it does not track hidden bad batches, inspection accuracy or recurring defects. Random origin attribution is a discussion device, not actual traceability. The revised rules require classroom piloting before claiming balanced difficulty.
